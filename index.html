Quantitative research · 2026→

A physical signal, tested against an economic one.

Snowpack → Hydro → Prices

A reproducible pipeline over 46 years of California snowpack and energy data. Snowpack tracks summer hydropower — +24.9 GWh per point (95% CI 5.0–44.8, p = 0.022) — but did not improve price forecasts over a simple baseline. The negative result is part of the finding.

  • 46 yrs of data
  • +24.9 GWh / point
  • 31 tests in CI
  • 4 forecast models

Quantitative research · 2026→

Volume tells you how much — not which way.

Activity ≠ Direction

13,000+ hourly SPY and Bitcoin bars, tested with permutation and bootstrap resampling. Trading activity predicts movement magnitude in all 4 datasets (permutation p ≤ 0.0002) — but shows no reliable link to direction. 64-setup sensitivity grid, walk-forward validation included.

  • 13,000+ hourly bars
  • 10,000-draw tests
  • 64 sensitivity setups
