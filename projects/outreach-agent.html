Portfolio Outreach Agent

Tell it who you’d like to meet. It does the research, checks its facts, and drafts the introductions.

See the code on GitHub ↗ ← Back to all projects

The problem, in plain words

Finding the right people to talk to is slow work. You search, open twenty tabs, copy names into a spreadsheet, then try to write a message that doesn’t sound like a robot wrote it.

Most tools that promise to help do one of two things: they hand you a huge list with no explanation of why anyone is on it, or they invent contact details that look plausible and aren’t real. Both waste your time — and the second one spends your reputation.

What it does

  1. It listens to the request.“We build robotics for construction and would like to meet general contractors.” The tool turns that sentence into a research plan.
  2. It searches, in parallel.Several researchers scour the web at once for real businesses — or real professionals — that fit the description.
  3. It keeps only what it can prove.Every detail has to appear in a real, cited source. If an email address or phone number can’t be verified, the field stays empty. Nothing is guessed.
  4. It ranks and writes.Candidates are scored on fit, and each one gets a short, personal message grounded in what was actually found — with the evidence attached.
  5. It hands you a package.A spreadsheet, a readable summary, and ready-to-send drafts you can open and send — after you’ve read them.

In everyday terms

The tool drafts. You decide. It exists to make the honest version of outreach faster — not the noisy version.

Why it matters

Good outreach is mostly research: knowing enough about someone to write something worth reading. That part takes hours and rarely gets the hours. Automating the search — while refusing to invent facts — turns a day of tab juggling into a short review session.

Who it’s for: founders looking for their first customers, investors supporting portfolio companies, and teams hiring for hard-to-find roles. The same pipeline works in both directions: describe the kind of professional you want to find, and it surfaces people and the companies that employ them.

Where it stands

It works today, straight from the command line. With no accounts or keys configured it runs on safe demonstration data, so you can see the entire workflow — research, scoring, drafts — before pointing it at real research.

What it is

A research-and-drafting assistant

The rule

Evidence, or it doesn’t ship

What you get

Spreadsheet + ready drafts

Who decides

You — before anything is sent

Good to know

  • It never guesses email addresses or phone numbers. A blank field means “not verified”, and stays blank.
  • A directory listing is never treated as a company’s own website.
  • Sending is a human decision: review every contact, and follow outreach laws and platform rules.
